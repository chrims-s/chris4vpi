Intro

Hey, I'm Chris, a 2nd year Computer Science and Commerce student. I’m currently one of the 2026 Human Resources Directors, alongside my fellow bums, Tori and Emma. This year, I’m running for Vice President of Internals for 2027.

About Me

I came into first year knowing almost no one. But regardless, I knew I really wanted to make the most of my university experience. You know, the fun, the adventures, the roadtrips, and everything else that EVERYONE always talks about. But, unfortunately, there wasn’t much of that at the start of my uni life.

That was until I joined CSESoc.

Being a part of CSESoc has genuinely been one of the best decisions I’ve made. Over the past two years, it’s given me opportunities to meet people and go to places I never would have otherwise. It’s introduced me to role models I look up to and aspire to become, and most importantly, it’s given me some of my closest friends, some who I’d happily call friends for life.

CSESoc has had such a meaningful impact on my university experience, and for everything it’s given me, I’d love for the opportunity to return the favour.

My Experience

Looking back, being part of the Human Resources subcom had always felt so special to me. I remember attending my first ever CSESoc event, Wheelchair Basketball, and this is where I really found my love for the society. No one in my port had gone, but the other ports attending welcomed me with open arms. And not only did they invite me to play basketball with them, but they also invited me out to dinner afterwards. It was through experiences like these where I found my place in CSESoc, and decided to give the society my all.

Hosting events as part of Human Resources has always been incredibly rewarding. Events like Picnic Day, Beach Day, and socwide roadtrip are all memories that I look back on fondly, not because of the activities themselves, but because of the people I met and the friendships I’ve made. Seeing people come together, talk, and form new friendships has always been one of the most rewarding aspects of HR, and at the end of the day, even though CSESoc is a society, it's also a place to make friends. And this belief has been a constant motivator throughout my time in CSE.

This year, I’ve had the opportunity to experience HR from the other side, as a director, and it has been just as fulfilling. Leading alongside co-directors who I was once subbies with, and watching them grow into the leaders they are today has been incredibly rewarding. But most importantly, leading my own subcom has been one of the most challenging yet fulfilling experiences of my CSE journey. We’ve already planned numerous HR events, including our movie night, Bounce (subcom induction event), and socwide roadtrip, and seeing the positive impacts these events have had on people has only reinforced my love for HR.

And ultimately, this is something that I’d love to continue fostering and supporting as part of the 2027 exec team.

Why VPI?

Ever since my first year in subcom, VPI has always been a role I’ve looked up to. Back then, it had always felt like such a distant role that I could only dream of being in. But now, having the opportunity to apply for it, I’ve realised that it’s a role I genuinely believe in.

Of course, the VPI has written responsibilities and duties, but I’ve come to realise that the role is much more than that. The current VPI has shown me that being VPI is about showing up and supporting the internal team, whether that means pulling up to events, lending a helping hand wherever necessary, or simply being someone the directors can turn to for guidance.

The current exec team has always been there to support us as directors, and I’d be privileged to do the same for the incoming director team. I want to be the same person who showed up for us, whether we needed it or not.

Visions for 2027

After having been in CSESoc for almost two years, I’ve seen firsthand what makes this society so special, but I’ve also noticed where it can continue to improve, and these experiences are what have shaped my visions and goals for CSESoc in 2027.

Fostering an inclusive and supportive internal environment where every member feels comfortable, valued, and motivated.

  • Encouraging a proactive culture where internal members actively show up for and support one another’s events, and moving away from a “sack/ceebs” culture
  • Ensuring all internal members know they can reach out for guidance, advice, or support whenever they need it
  • Working closely with the Director team to ensure they are consistently supported throughout the year

Cultivating a strong inter-connected community where CSESoc feels like one group of friends rather than a collection of disconnected ports.

  • Creating and encouraging more opportunities for inter-portflio collaboration and bonding through internal socwide events
  • Establishing clear communication channels between portfolios and across the broader society such that interportfolio communication feels more natural, comfortable, and accessible
